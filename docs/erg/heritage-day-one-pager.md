## Heritage Day — Winter Light

**Bring the season to your people.**

Winter Light is a culturally competent holiday kit for families with teens: make-together crafts and party plans for the hosts, a seven-night family practice built on the Nguzo Saba for the ones who want the season to mean something, and a bundle for households that want both. Heritage Day puts a Winter Light Complete kit in every household in your ERG and gathers them once, families included, before the break. It is built on twenty years of research into how young people keep their brilliance intact, and it is the one thing on our list that is available right now.

**What the ERG gets**

- A family-inclusive Heritage Day gathering, December through February. `[ERICA: on-site, virtual, or both for the Day; the site says "on-site or virtual" for the Hour only]`
- A Winter Light Complete kit for every household: Make and Practice, in print; your teen designs the eighth night; a private family readout after the season. `[ERICA: Complete at $88, or $118 with one handmade object from a Black maker and the maker's story]`
- Inside Make: a build-your-own kinara craft with templates and materials list; seven table-card crafts, one per night; a party run-of-show for a Kwanzaa or winter-break gathering; playlist, menu cards, gift tags.
- Inside Practice: the Nguzo Saba seven-night family practice deck, prompts written with teens; the Firefly Journal, 16 pages; the Soul Compass family check-in; a local and virtual events guide.
- Every kit passes six standards before it ships: safety is the first feature; representation in every touchpoint; heritage as content, not costume; teen agency, peer register; neurodivergent-first; measured, with the data returned to families.
- The smaller door: a Heritage Hour, 60–90 minutes, on-site or virtual, with digital kits for everyone who attends. One chair can approve it.

## Heritage Day — Pride ERG, December edition

**Everybody who's coming is already welcome.**

Whole Table is a set of Kwanzaa and winter-break kits for Black families where LGBTQIA+ people are at the table: the affirming host, the adult child coming home, the couple hosting chosen family, the LGBTQIA+ elder carrying legacy. Heritage Day, December edition, puts a Whole Table Complete kit in every household in your Pride ERG and runs one gathering on the Open House plan, where nobody is introduced as anything and anyone can pass. Nothing in it asks anyone to come out, be outed, or explain themselves, and that includes to their employer.

**What the ERG gets**

- One family-inclusive gathering run on the Open House run-of-show: a 40-second welcome ("everyone's name, everyone's person"), pronoun-optional name tags where the second line is blank for everybody, seven table cards that read the Nguzo Saba through community, and three short scripts for the relative who says something. No speech. `[ERICA: on-site, virtual, or both]`
- Whole Table Complete for every household ($99 digital): Open House (for hosts and gift-givers), Coming Home (adult children returning, and the parents receiving them), Chosen Family Table (couples and friends hosting chosen family), Elder Light (older LGBTQIA+ adults and the younger people who love them), plus a family Soul Compass readout after the season.
- The affirming family lens, +$0, on every kit. A household that would rather have Winter Light Make or Practice, or any Wellness Season kit, ticks one box and gets a four-page insert of affirming prompts matched to that kit, the host scripts from Open House, and discreet-delivery mode. A checkbox, not an upsell.
- No disclosure, to anyone. Each household chooses its kit and whether to add the lens with us, on our site, not on a list the ERG collects. We never tell the ERG or the employer who chose what. The ally, the parent of a queer kid, and the cousin nobody's sure about are covered by the same rule: nobody has to say why they want it. `[ERICA: confirm the per-household claim-code flow so the ERG buys a block and households pick their own kit]`

## Heritage Day — Wellness ERG, January edition

**The one time of year everyone's already in the room.**

Wellness Season is six kits that treat Thanksgiving through New Year as the one window the whole family is already gathered: nourishment, mind, steadiness, the teen's own room, family weather, and legacy across generations. Heritage Day, January edition, puts a Wellness Season Complete kit in every household in your wellness ERG and gathers them once in the new year, with the season the kits were built for just behind them. These are practices with a Black cultural root named for each, not treatment: no diet language, no weight talk, no diagnostic framing, no "fix your family," and no claims about what happens after.

**What the ERG gets**

- One family-inclusive gathering built from the kits: a Family Weather check-in (say your weather, not your reasons), a Mind Lanterns games round elders win, and one Legacy Table story prompt. Every piece has a time estimate, a sensory note, and a low-demand version. `[ERICA: on-site, virtual, or both]`
- Wellness Season Complete for every household ($79 digital), all six kits plus a family Soul Compass readout after the season:
  - **Nourish** ($24, nutritional): cook-together plans built on Black holiday food as it is, not "healthified," one small adaptation per dish offered as a choice; a "who taught you this" recipe-archive card; a grocery list that splits by generation so the teen owns a dish.
  - **Mind Lanterns** ($19, cognitive): intergenerational games night; a "brain myths" card; a phone-down hour; an eighth-night puzzle the teen designs.
  - **Steady** ($29, psychological): regulation practices done together (breath, movement, quiet); a grief-at-the-holidays card for the empty chair; an overwhelm exit plan for anyone; Soul Compass check-in.
  - **Teen Room** ($19, adolescent): written to the teen, not the parent; a "my terms for the holidays" one-pager the teen fills and shares, or doesn't; a low-demand participation menu; the eighth night is theirs to design.
  - **Family Weather** ($29, family dynamics): the weather-report check-in; repair scripts; a boundaries card for hosts and guests; a way to end a conversation without ending the relationship.
  - **Legacy Table** ($34, intergenerational): oral-history interview kit; a family tree that includes chosen family; recipe and photo archive prompts; a legacy letter; a "what we're carrying forward" Kuumba night.
- Every kit carries a "this isn't therapy" card: a plain line about when to reach for a professional, and verified warmlines (988 call or text; Call BlackLine 1-800-604-5841; Trans Lifeline 877-565-8860, weekdays 1–9 PM ET).
- The affirming family lens, +$0, on every Wellness kit, with discreet-delivery mode. Households choose it with us; the ERG never sees who did.
- Seven standards: the six above, plus no clinical creep.
