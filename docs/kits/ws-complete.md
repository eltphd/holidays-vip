## Before you open this (read this first)

Six kits, one family, Thanksgiving to New Year. These are practices, not treatment; every kit names where each one comes from and doesn't promise what it'll do. The house rule across all six: **anyone can pass.** Each kit carries this card; it's here too because the bundle gets opened first:

> **This isn't therapy.** These are practices, not treatment. We name where each one comes from; we don't promise what it will do. If someone at your table needs more than a practice, reach for a professional. If it's heavier than a practice can hold, these are people who pick up: **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline** — call or text 988, any hour. **Call BlackLine** — 1-800-604-5841, peer support by us, for us, with an LGBTQ+ Black femme lens. **Trans Lifeline** — 877-565-8860, run by trans people, for trans people, weekdays 1–9 PM Eastern.

**What this asks of you**
- Time and sensory: each kit says its own; nothing in the season requires touch, a loud room, or talking in a group. Nobody has to do all six; three done well is a season.
- Low-demand version: open the kit whose week it is, do its bare minimum, close it.

## 3. The Family Soul Compass readout

**What it is.** One page the family fills at the end of the season, from the check-ins the kits already carry (Steady's Soul Compass check-in, Family Weather's weather reports, Legacy Table's family readout). Not a new activity; the family looking back at what it already wrote down. A few prompts, and anyone can pass:

- The weather we had most this season, in a word.
- One thing a kit had us do that we're keeping.
- One thing we're dropping.
- The names we said out loud.

Time: fifteen minutes, once. Sensory: paper, a pen, one person reading it back. Low-demand: one line, any prompt.

**What it is not.** Not a survey, a score, or a measurement of anybody. Nothing individual leaves the house: not the teen's one-pager, not the journal, not the elder's recording, not anybody's weather report.

The aggregate season readout from Measurement Ally is separate: an invitation after the season, only with consent, only what the family chooses, and saying no changes nothing.

**How it's delivered.** Each kit arrives on its date by its own email link, its own card at the front. The readout is one page in the Dec 12 files: fill it on paper or on screen, keep it in the house, never send it back. The Measurement Ally invitation, if any, comes after January 1 by separate email and says plainly what it's asking.
