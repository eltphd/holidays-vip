# holidayz.vip — Catalog Dev Build v1: Whole Table (LGBTQIA+) + Wellness Season

**Prepared:** September 20, 2026 · **Owner:** Erica Tartt (Altered Earth Press / US-Squared Research Institute)
**Decisions this build encodes:** ship this season, digital-first (print deferred to 2027); LGBTQIA+ is both a standalone category and an affirming-family lens on every kit.
**Companion:** `supabase/seed/holidayz-catalog-seed.json`.

## What this is

Two new catalog categories on holidayz.vip, sold alongside Winter Light through Dec 31:

- **Whole Table** — Kwanzaa/winter-break kits for Black families where LGBTQIA+ people are at the table: the affirming host, the adult child coming home, the couple hosting chosen family, the LGBTQIA+ elder carrying legacy. Plus an **affirming-family lens** (a variant flag) that adds a prompt insert and hosting notes to any Winter Light or Wellness kit.
- **Wellness Season** — Thanksgiving → New Year kits that treat the holidays as the one window the whole family is already gathered: nourishment, mind, steadiness, the teen's own room, family weather, and legacy across generations.

## Why now

The holidays are when a queer adult child decides whether to go home, when a trans teen finds out whether the table is safe, when a family with an elder in decline realizes this may be the last season to record the stories. Mainstream "inclusive holiday" content is corporate and white; Black affirming-family content at the household level barely exists as product. Wellness content is everywhere and almost never intergenerational, culturally rooted, or built for a teen who won't do anything that feels like school.

## The six design standards (every kit)

1. **Safety first** — nothing asks anyone to disclose, perform, or defend; opt-outs are real.
2. **Representation** — the people in the copy and art are us; no tokens, no costume.
3. **Heritage as content** — the Nguzo Saba / Black holiday tradition is the operating system, not decoration.
4. **Peer register** — a teen or an elder would say this out loud; nothing reads as school or HR.
5. **Neurodivergent-first** — time, sensory, low-demand version present and honest.
6. **Measured, returned** — the Soul Compass check-in feels like a gift to the family, not surveillance.

Whole Table adds **7. Outing risk** — a hostile relative seeing this kit, its email, or its file names learns nothing.
Wellness Season adds **8. No clinical creep** — no diagnosis, weight, or outcome claims; the "not therapy" card is honest.

## Category B — Wellness Season

**Tagline:** *The one time of year everyone's already in the room.*
**Category rule:** these are practices, not treatment. No diet language, no weight talk, no diagnostic framing, no "fix your family." Every kit carries a **"this isn't therapy" card** with a plain line about when to reach for a professional and verified warmlines (988 call/text; Call BlackLine 1-800-604-5841; Trans Lifeline 877-565-8860, weekdays 1–9 PM ET). ## Soul Compass (what it is, in every kit that carries it)

A short family check-in the family keeps. It's a gift back to the household, not surveillance: nobody's individual answers leave the house; what the family chooses to share (aggregate, consented) feeds a season readout from Measurement Ally after the season. Kits describe it as a practice, not a measurement instrument, and never promise outcomes.
