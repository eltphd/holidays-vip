# Recruiting and briefing the community builders — emails and scripts

All in Erica's first person. Swap names. Keep them short; long is a debate.

## 1. The invitation (send Sept 22–24)

**Subject:** Would you read something for me before December?

Hi [Name],

I'm building holiday kits for our families this season, under holidayz.vip. Two of them are about us: queer folks at Black family tables, and the holidays being the one time everyone's already in the room.

I'd rather you tell me it's wrong now than a stranger tell me in December.

Here's the ask. You read drafts twice, once in mid-October and once at the end of October. Each time you read as yourself and mark anything that would make you put it down, anything that asks somebody to explain themselves, and anything that's just not how we talk. There's a short scorecard; your margin notes matter more. About two hours each round.

You'd be credited as a community builder if you want to be, or not at all. $75 or one of the kit bundles, your pick.

Can I have 20 minutes on a call next week to walk you through it? Or I can send a voice memo if that's easier.

Erica

---

## 2. Guardian version (for the adolescent seat; send to the parent, not the teen)

**Subject:** An invitation for [Teen's name], with your okay first

Hi [Parent's name],

I'd like [Teen] to read something for me. I'm building holiday kits for Black families with teens, and one of them is written to the teen, not the parent. I need a real teen to tell me where it sounds like an adult trying.

What it involves: reading drafts twice in October, about two hours each time, and marking what's embarrassing, what asks them to share something they wouldn't, and what's just not how they talk. No sessions are recorded. Anything we discuss happens with you aware and reachable. Compensation ($75 or a kit) goes to you or as a kit, never as a direct payment to [Teen].

If that's okay with you, I'll send the one-page consent for you both to sign, and we'll find 20 minutes to talk it through with you on the call.

Erica

---

## 3. Voice-memo script (if you'd rather record than call; about 3 minutes)

Read `auditor-brief.md` "The ask" verbatim, then add:

> Two dates. Drafts come to you October 12 and October 27. You've got about a week each time. There's a scorecard with eight lines, zero to two. A zero means "must fix" and it stops the kit until we fix it. That's real power, and it's the reason I'm asking you and not a focus group.
>
> I'll send the consent form after this. It's one page. Pick your credit and pick your pay. If you're under eighteen, your parent signs too. Reply to the email with your choices, or print it and send me a photo. Either counts.
>
> That's it. Thank you for even listening to this.

---

## 4. Follow-up after the call (send the same day)

**Subject:** What I said, in writing, plus the one page to sign

Hi [Name],

Thank you for today. Here's everything in one place.

**The consent form** is attached. To sign it, reply to this email with your four answers:
1. Credit: your full name / first name only / don't list me
2. Compensation: $75 / a Complete bundle (which one: Winter Light, Whole Table, or Wellness Season) / neither
3. "I've read the consent page and I agree." (typed is fine)
4. If you're under 18, have your parent reply from their own email with the same line.

That reply is your signature. If you'd rather print and sign, a photo works.

**The scorecard** is attached so you know what's coming. You don't need to do anything with it yet.

**Dates:** drafts arrive October 12 and October 27. About a week each.

**Who you're reading for:** [fill from the lens table: e.g., "You own Chosen Family Table, and I'd love your eyes on Open House and the lens insert too."]

Reply by October 4 if you can. If you can't, tell me and we'll figure it out.

Erica

*Attach: `consent-form.md` (export to PDF), `scorecard.md` (export to PDF).*
