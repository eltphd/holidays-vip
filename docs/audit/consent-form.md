# Community Builder Consent — holidayz.vip, Winter Light season 2026

**Who's asking:** Erica Tartt, Altered Earth Press, a program of US-Squared Research Institute (501(c)(3)).
**What this is:** one page, plain language. Sign it or don't. Either answer is fine.

## What you'd be reviewing

Draft holiday kits for two new categories on holidayz.vip:

- **Whole Table** — Kwanzaa and winter-break kits for Black families where LGBTQIA+ people are at the table.
- **Wellness Season** — Thanksgiving-to-New-Year practices for the whole family, written so a teen or an elder would say them out loud.

You'll read drafts twice: once in mid-October and once at the end of October. Each time you read as yourself. You mark anything that would make you put it down, anything that asks somebody to explain themselves, and anything that's just not how we talk. There's a short scorecard. Your margin notes matter more.

## How your notes are used

- Your notes change the kits. That's the point.
- Your notes are stored with your first name or chosen name and the lens you're reading through (for example "older adult" or "trans"). Your email and phone stay in Erica's private sheet and never go in the database.
- Nothing you write is quoted publicly without asking you first.
- No sessions are recorded.
