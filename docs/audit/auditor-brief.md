# Auditor brief — what to say on the call (about 20 minutes)

This is the script from the spec, in Erica's words, plus the logistics people will ask about. Say it, or send it as a voice memo.

## The ask (in your words)

> I'm building holiday kits for our families. Two of them are about us — queer folks at Black family tables — and about the holidays being the one time everyone's in the room. I'd rather you tell me it's wrong now than a stranger tell me in December.
>
> You'll get drafts twice. Each time: read it as yourself, mark anything that would make you put it down, anything that asks somebody to explain themselves, and anything that's just not how we talk. There's a short scorecard but your margin notes matter more.
>
> You'll be credited as a community builder if you want to be, or not at all. Honorarium or the kits, your pick.

## The scorecard, in one breath

Eight lines, each scored 0, 1, or 2. 0 means "must fix" and stops the kit. 1 means "works with edits." 2 means "ready." Whole Table kits get an extra line for outing risk; Wellness kits get an extra line for clinical creep. See `scorecard.md`.

## What to send back after the call

The consent form (`consent-form.md`), signed, by Oct 4. For anyone under 18, a guardian signs too.
